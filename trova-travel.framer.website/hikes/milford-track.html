Milford Track

Four days through fjordland rainforest and over an alpine pass, ending at Milford Sound — often called the finest walk in the world.

Four days through fjordland rainforest and over an alpine pass, ending at Milford Sound — often called the finest walk in the world.

Moderate

4–8 people

4 Days

53.5 km

Overview

A point-to-point walk through native rainforest, river valley, and a high alpine pass, finishing at one of the most photographed fjords on Earth. Both ends are boat-access only — there's no road in or out, which keeps the trail uncrowded despite its reputation.Huts are booked out months ahead during the main season. The reward is a single sustained walk-in to Milford Sound rather than a day-trip crowd seeing it from a bus.

Hike Schedule

Day 1 — Boat across the lake to the trailhead, short walk through native forest to first hut

Day 2 — Valley and forest walking along the river toward the base of the pass

Day 3 — Climb over the alpine pass, waterfalls on the descent into the next valley

Day 4 — Final valley walk to Milford Sound, boat departure
